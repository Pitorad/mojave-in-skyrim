**Skyrim, scored by Fallout: New Vegas.** You play Skyrim as usual; the Mojave does the soundtrack. Single player.

### The Mojave bleeds in
Skyrim still decides what a moment needs, and New Vegas answers it with its own music, read from your installed copy of Fallout: New Vegas:
- **Wilds:** desert and mountain exploration tracks (the Jacobstown lodge theme in the snow)
- **Towns:** Freeside and Mojave settlement music
- **Dungeons, caves and forts:** vault and cave music
- **Fights:** New Vegas battle loops; bosses and dragons get its darker ones
- **Stingers** for discoveries, rewards and death
- **Day and night** versions follow Skyrim's clock
- The **main menu and the Helgen cart ride** play New Vegas's main title

Story moments (the Word Wall chant, Sovngarde, the Elder Scroll, Alduin's defeat and a few others) keep Skyrim's own music.

### Radio New Vegas
Press **N** to switch it on or off. You get the station's songs, from "Big Iron" to "Ain't That a Kick in the Head", and Mr. New Vegas himself: a hello when you tune in, song intros, and the news every few songs. Each song's title shows up on screen.

### Good to know
- Needs **Fallout: New Vegas** installed on the same PC. Nothing from it is in the download: every song and line comes from your own copy while Skyrim runs.
- Plays on Skyrim SE/AE **1.6.1170 to 1.7.104**. It loads through Ultimate ASI Loader (Melty installs it), with no SKSE needed.
- Your Master and Music volume sliders still apply. Alt-tab and it fades out.
- Settings (radio key, loudness, how often the news comes on, day/night hours) live in `MojaveInSkyrim.ini` next to `SkyrimSE.exe`.
